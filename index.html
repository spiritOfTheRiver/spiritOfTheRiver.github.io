Respaldo Pi

Respaldo Pi is a personal backup tool that automatically copies the files of a Raspberry Pi to its owner's own Google Drive account. It is a private, single-user tool and is not offered as a public service.

What it does

  • Once a day, it scans the home directory of the owner's Raspberry Pi and uploads new or changed files to a folder in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Files are encrypted on the Raspberry Pi before they are uploaded, so their contents and names are not readable in Google Drive.
  • Previous versions of changed or deleted files are kept for 30 days so they can be restored.
  • It uses the open-source program rclone as its upload engine.

Why it requests access to Google Drive

Respaldo Pi requests permission to access Google Drive for one purpose only: to create, update, list and delete the backup files it stores in the owner's Drive, and to read them back when the owner restores a backup. It does not read, analyze or modify any other content, and it does not access any other Google service or any personal information.

Privacy

Respaldo Pi (español)

Respaldo Pi es una herramienta personal que copia automáticamente los archivos de una Raspberry Pi a la cuenta de Google Drive de su propietario. Es de uso privado y no es un servicio público.

  • Una vez al día sube a una carpeta del Google Drive del propietario los archivos nuevos o modificados de su Raspberry Pi.
  • Los archivos se cifran en la Raspberry Pi antes de subirse.
  • Se conservan durante 30 días las versiones anteriores de archivos modificados o borrados.

Solicita acceso a Google Drive únicamente para crear, actualizar, listar, eliminar y leer sus propios archivos de respaldo. No accede a ningún otro contenido ni a otros servicios de Google. Consulta la Política de privacidad.
